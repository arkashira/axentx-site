How it works · the sovereign loop

Local by default. Verified by design.

Every request runs on your model first. When a task truly needs the frontier, it leaves through a gate you can read — and returns one answer, checked.

01 · LOCAL

Run on your model

The domain model answers on your own GPUs. The default path never touches an external network.

02 · GATE

No-train egress

If the frontier is needed, only providers that won't train on your data are eligible — enforced, not requested.

03 · FAN-OUT

Council of specialists

Several models propose in parallel; a confidence-weighted judge resolves disagreement into one answer.

04 · AUDIT

Every byte accounted for

You get the verified result and a full record of exactly what left the machine, and to whom.

> The harness decides when a task is done — deepen, verify, and claim gates keep a small local model working long past where a single call would stop.
