Why sovereign

You should not have to trust us. You should be able to verify us.

A company born this year cannot out-trust one you already know. So we removed the need. Unplug the network and Surrogate still reads your code, runs your tests, and fixes what breaks.

Nothing leaves the machine unless you route it out yourself, and the egress gate shows you every byte when you do. Regulated and air-gapped teams cannot hand their source to a cloud they do not control. Surrogate is built for exactly that constraint.

Inspectable, not a brand you take on faith. Run it disconnected and watch.
What we build

One model. One agent. Yours to run.

Surrogate OneThe model

A DevSecOps and SRE model you host yourself. Specialized on the work your team actually does, and small enough to serve on your own GPU.

Surrogate CodeThe agent

A terminal and TUI engineering agent. It works across your stack on the model you host, and asks before anything leaves the box.

SentinelManaged

The same agent as a service that watches your infrastructure around the clock and acts inside the authority you set, per service and per action.

ArkshipThe core

The orchestration runtime every Axentx agent runs on, with a tiered authority model built once and inherited everywhere.
