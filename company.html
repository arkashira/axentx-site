Company

Built in Thailand, for the teams a foreign cloud cannot serve.

Axentx is a deep-tech company in Bangkok. We build an engineering agent and a model for teams that run regulated or air-gapped systems, where the code an agent reads can never reach a network the customer does not control.

Why now

The most capable agents cannot go where this work lives.

A coding and monitoring agent works best with full context: your source, your secrets, your telemetry. For a bank, a hospital, or a government team, sending that to a foreign cloud is not a hard decision. It is simply not allowed.

So the frontier agents are off the table for exactly the teams that most need one. A model small enough to self-host, specialized on their domain, closes that gap. Open weights and cheaper inference make it practical now, on a single GPU.

We do not ask you to trust a newcomer. We make trust unnecessary. The model runs in your walls, and you audit what leaves.
